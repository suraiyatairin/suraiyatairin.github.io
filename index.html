Suraiya Tairin, Ph.D.

Tenure-track Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science

Prairie View A&M University

My research interests lie in AI/ML systems and applied AI, with an emphasis on developing efficient, scalable, reliable, and practical AI solutions. My systems research spans generative AI, LLMs, efficient LLM training and inference, Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) inference, ML job scheduling, Systems for ML/ML for Systems, Systems for LLMs, distributed and parallel systems, cloud computing, and high-performance computing.

My applied research extends to agentic AI, computer vision, multimodal AI, video analysis, data mining, and data science for real-world problems in transportation, infrastructure, road safety, and agriculture.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from the University of Virginia. I received my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology.
